Delete your ONEDRFUL account

You can delete your account and the data tied to it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open ONEDRFUL and tap Me.
  2. Scroll to Data and privacy.
  3. Tap Delete account and data, then Delete everything.

Without the app

Email support@onedrful.app from the email or phone number on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We confirm within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, account type, business name and contact details.
  • Your payout preference.
  • Your referral records, uploaded proof files and the offers you posted.

What we may keep

Anonymous totals used for business trust scores, and records we must keep for legal, tax or fraud-prevention reasons, for no longer than required (normally up to 90 days for fraud review). Everything else is removed within 30 days.
