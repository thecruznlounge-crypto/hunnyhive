Privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026 · Operated by [Your legal business name], Miami, Florida, USA

ONEDRFUL is a referral marketplace. Businesses post referral payouts, individuals share tracked links and QR codes, and businesses pay those individuals directly. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name, account type (individual or business), business name, and email or phone if you register.
  • Payout preference — the method and handle you choose (for example a Cash App tag or Zelle email). We store it so an approving business knows where to pay you. We do not collect bank account or card numbers and we never hold payouts.
  • Referral records — the offers you share, link clicks, customer claim notes, approval status and payout status.
  • Proof files — receipts or photos you choose to upload as proof of a sale.
  • Offers — the payouts, descriptions and terms a business posts.
  • Basic device data — information your browser sends with each request (such as IP address and device type), used for security and to keep the service running.

We do not use advertising SDKs, we do not sell personal information, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run the referral proof trail: link, claim, proof, approval and payment.
  • To show a business only the referrals tied to its own offers, and to show promoters only their own activity.
  • To calculate public payout trust scores for businesses (based on payout speed and disputes, never on promoter identity).
  • To review reported offers and resolve disputes.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent fraud.

What is stored on your device

The app saves your data on your device so it works offline. Clearing the app’s storage or using Delete account and data in the app removes it.

Who can see your information

  • Businesses see your name, referral activity and payout preference only for referrals tied to their own offers.
  • Promoters see a business’s public offer details and trust score.
  • Service providers that host our servers and storage process data on our behalf under contract.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

Retention

We keep account and referral records while your account is open. When you delete your account we delete your personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal, tax or fraud-prevention reasons, which are kept no longer than required.

Your choices

  • Download a copy of your data in the app: Me → Download my data.
  • Delete your account in the app: Me → Delete account and data, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Ask us to correct or delete information by emailing support@onedrful.app.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Access to production data is limited to staff who need it to run the service.

Children

ONEDRFUL is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
