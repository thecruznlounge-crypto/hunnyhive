What ONEDRFUL does

ONEDRFUL lets businesses post referral payouts and lets individuals share tracked links and QR codes for those offers. ONEDRFUL records the proof trail (link, customer claim, proof, approval and payment status). ONEDRFUL does not hold, send or guarantee payouts. Each business pays promoters directly under the terms of its own offer.

Eligibility

You must be at least 18 and able to enter a binding agreement.

Businesses

  • You agree to review referrals in good faith and pay approved referrals as promised.
  • Payout speed and disputes are shown publicly as your trust score.

Promoters

  • Share offers honestly. No spam, fake leads, self-referrals or misleading claims.
  • Follow advertising and disclosure rules where you share links (for example, saying you may earn a referral fee).

Disputes

If a business rejects a referral you believe is valid, you can open a dispute in the app. We review the proof trail and may adjust trust scores, but the payment decision and obligation remain between the promoter and the business.
