List your PWA in 3 steps

No account, no zip files, no forms about things we can read from your app. Add one line, paste your URL, done. You keep hosting your app on your own domain, or we can host it for you under a slug or subdomain.

1. Add Quick Install (required)

Every app in the store should install in one tap. Apps without it are still listed, but marked Preview until they add it. When someone taps Install in the store, we open your app with ?install=1. The SDK catches that and shows a single full-screen Install button that fires the browser’s native install dialog. It also gives iPhone users a two-step “Add to Home Screen” guide.

Paste this as high as you can in your <head>, before your other scripts:

<script src="https://hunnyhive.app/sdk/install.js"></script>

Optional: give any button data-hunnyhive-install and it becomes a one-tap install button inside your own UI. (Already using data-mypwa-install from mypwa.store? It still works.)

<button data-hunnyhive-install>Install app</button>

Already built your own install flow? That works too, as long as your app handles ?install=1 by offering a one-tap install. We check for it.

2. Make sure the basics are in place

  • Served over HTTPS.
  • A web app manifest with name, start_url and "display": "standalone".
  • PNG icons at 192×192 and 512×512, plus a 512×512 maskable icon (recommended).
  • A service worker so it opens offline (recommended).
  • A description and a few screenshots in the manifest (recommended: the store shows them on your preview page).
{
  "name": "My App",
  "short_name": "My App",
  "description": "One sentence about what it does.",
  "start_url": "/",
  "scope": "/",
  "display": "standalone",
  "theme_color": "#5b4bf0",
  "background_color": "#ffffff",
  "icons": [
    { "src": "/icons/192.png", "sizes": "192x192", "type": "image/png", "purpose": "any" },
    { "src": "/icons/512.png", "sizes": "512x512", "type": "image/png", "purpose": "any" },
    { "src": "/icons/maskable-512.png", "sizes": "512x512", "type": "image/png", "purpose": "maskable" }
  ]
}

3. Check it, then submit it

Paste your address to see exactly what passes and what needs a fix. It reads your live site, nothing is stored.

What we do with your submission

  • We read your manifest to fill in the name, description, icon and screenshots. You only provide the URL and an email.
  • Your icon is resized to our standard (512×512, full-bleed). Provide a square icon with no transparent margin for the best result.
  • A person looks at it, picks the category, and lists it. You’ll get a preview page at hunnyhive.app/apps/your-app.
  • Your icon, name and description always come from your own manifest, so updates on your side show up after a refresh by us.

Hosting options

  • Your domain (most common): we list it and send people straight to you.
  • hunnyhive.app/your-slug or your-slug.hunnyhive.app: we host the files. Ask us in the submit form note.
  • Redirect wrapper: a tiny installable shell on Hunnyhive that opens your site.
