Bloop

Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026 · bloopr.space

The short version

  • No account, email or real name is needed to use Bloop.
  • Your location is used to find restrooms near you. Bloop doesn't save it or keep a history of where you've been.
  • No ads, no ad tracking, and we never sell your information.
  • Things you choose to post (ratings, reviews, check-ins, access tips, photos, restrooms you add, problem reports) are shared with other Bloop users.

What Bloop stores

On your device: a random device ID, your settings, saved restrooms, your own ratings and check-ins, and recent search results so the app works offline. You can erase all of it any time in Account → Clear my data.

On our servers (only when you post something):

  • Ratings, written reviews, and the display name you choose (or "Anonymous").
  • Check-ins (open, closed or out of order) and problem reports.
  • Tips on how to get in, like where to find the key or a door code you share.
  • Restrooms you add, including the location you pinned and any details you typed.
  • Photos you upload. Photos are reviewed before they're shown to others.

Each post is linked to your random device ID, which we use to limit spam and abuse. It isn't linked to your name, phone number or email.

Your location

When you allow location access, Bloop uses it on your device to sort restrooms by distance and to guide you there. To look up nearby restrooms, addresses and directions, the app sends map coordinates (not your identity) to these OpenStreetMap services:

  • Overpass API: finding restrooms near a spot on the map
  • Nominatim: searching places and addresses
  • OSRM routing servers (FOSSGIS): walking and driving directions
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers: the map itself

These services receive your device's internet address like any website does, and follow their own privacy policies. Bloop doesn't save your location on its servers. The only location we store is the spot you pin when you add a restroom.

Who we share data with

Your posts are stored with our database provider, Supabase, and shown publicly in the app. We don't share or sell information to advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law.

Keeping and deleting data

Posts stay up until they're removed for breaking our Terms or you ask us to delete them. To delete your posts, email us with the device ID shown in Account → About, and we'll remove everything linked to it.

Children

Bloop isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions or deletion requests: support@bloopr.space
