1. What Merkuri is

Merkuri is a technology platform that lets customers and businesses find, compare and book independent couriers and delivery fleets, and gives couriers and fleets tools to run their delivery businesses. Merkuri is not a delivery or transportation carrier and does not employ couriers. Couriers and fleets are independent businesses that choose which requests to accept and set their own rates.
