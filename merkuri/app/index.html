About Merkuri

Merkuri is delivery infrastructure built in South Florida for the people who actually move things — independent couriers, small fleets and local merchants. Customers keep the power of choice: compare real couriers, watch their videos, compare rates and pick who delivers. Payments sit in escrow until proof is captured.
