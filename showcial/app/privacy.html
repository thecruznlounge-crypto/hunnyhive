Privacy & Terms

Last updated: November 2026

What we store

Showcial currently stores everything on your own device (browser local storage): the artists you create, your votes, wishlist and any reports you send. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and there are no accounts, ads or tracking cookies.

AI-generated content

Every artist, avatar and song in Showcial is generated by the app's own synthesis engine. Artists must be original: names or content that imitate a real performer or person are not allowed. Use the ⚑ report button on any artist page to flag content.

Downloads and purchases

Demo tracks can be downloaded for free for personal use. Paid full-length tracks are not yet on sale; items you save go to a wishlist on your device only.

Demo season

The current season runs locally on your device, and fan votes for the demo artists are simulated so you can experience the full show offline.

Deleting your data

Use Charts → Host tools → Reset everything, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
