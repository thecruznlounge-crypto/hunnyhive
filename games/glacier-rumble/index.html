How to play

  • Drag back from anywhere and release to slide, like a slingshot. A longer pull is a harder shove.
  • Slam into rivals to send them toward the edge. Heavier animals are harder to move.
  • The ice melts inward. An orange warning shows where the next piece breaks off.
  • Tap your special move button. It has its own cooldown.
  • Last animal on the ice wins the round. First to 2 round wins takes the match.
