How to play

  • Draw a fighter and a weapon, or tap a ready-made design. More ink means more health but less speed. Long weapons reach far; thick ones hit hard.
  • Duel a rival (best of 3), Rumble through waves, or Melee against three rivals.
  • Buttons: move, Jump (twice in the air), Hit swings your weapon, Ink fires a ranged blob.
  • Get knocked off the stage and you are out.
