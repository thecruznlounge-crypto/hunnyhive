How to play

  • Memory: a shape flashes for a few seconds, then it is gone. Sketch it from memory.
  • Trace: the shape stays as a faint guide. Follow it as neatly as you can.
  • Two players: both sketch the same shapes on one device. Highest total wins.
  • Size and position do not matter. Only the shape does.
