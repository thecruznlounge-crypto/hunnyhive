How to play

  • Pour: drag to pour sand. Loose sand slides into a pile.
  • Pack: drag over sand to wet and pack it. Packed sand holds against the waves.
  • Flag: tap the flag button, then tap the top of your castle to plant it.
  • When the tide comes, the flag must still stand above the line. Waves wash loose sand away first.
