How to play

  • Every round shows several pools. Only one is real.
  • Mirages shimmer, have wrong reflections, or lack a sandy shore. Look closely.
  • Tap the real water. Wrong guesses cost a lot of your water.
  • Each round the fakes get better, and your timer gets shorter.
