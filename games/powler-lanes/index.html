How to play

  • Use the Position slider to choose where the snowball starts.
  • Drag back from the ball and let go. A longer pull is more power, and sliding sideways changes the angle.
  • The Curve slider bends the ball. The dotted preview shows where it will go.
  • Slick patches push the ball sideways. Grab fish for a speed boost. Open water on the sides is a gutter.
  • Ten frames, two rolls each. Strikes and spares earn bonus points. Your special roll can be used twice a game.
