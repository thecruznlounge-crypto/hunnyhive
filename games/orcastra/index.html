How to play

  • Bubbles rise toward the glowing line in four lanes.
  • Tap the lane as a bubble reaches the line. Keys: D F J K.
  • Every hit plays a note, so you are the lead melody over the band.
  • Keep your combo going to raise the multiplier. Misses drain the crowd meter; empty it and the show ends.
  • If hits feel early or late, change Sync in the pause menu.
