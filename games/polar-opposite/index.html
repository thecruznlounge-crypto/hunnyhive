How to play

  • Swipe (or use the arrows). The polar bear goes your way, the penguin goes the opposite way.
  • Walls stop an animal; the other one still moves. Water is fatal, so plan around it.
  • Put the bear on its orange ring and the penguin on its teal ring at the same time.
  • Fewer moves earn more stars. Undo is free.
