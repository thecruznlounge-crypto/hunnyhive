How to play

  • Swipe (or tap the left or right half) to change lanes.
  • Drive through the items on your ticket. Items that are not on it cost points.
  • When the ticket is full, steer into the pickup window lane.
  • Cones, cars and trucks cost a life. Puddles make you slide. Three crashes ends the run.
