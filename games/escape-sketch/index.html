SKETCH YOUR WAY OUTEscape Sketch

Draw ladders, ropes, planks, tunnels and vehicles. The prisoner walks on his own. Help him reach the exit.

Levels

How to play

  • Pick a tool and draw on the level. Your ink is limited.
  • Ladder / Rope: climb walls. Ropes must hang from something. Plank: a bridge or ramp.
  • Tunnel digs rock. Cutters clear bars. Vehicle: sketch a car and it carries the prisoner right.
  • Press Go. He walks right, climbs, and falls on his own. Less ink used earns more stars.
