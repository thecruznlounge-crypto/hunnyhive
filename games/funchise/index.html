How to play

  • Tap a lot, then open or upgrade a store. Stores earn money every second.
  • Tap the smiley bubbles for tips. Managers collect some for you.
  • Events happen: tap the inspector in time and restock stores that run out.
  • Reach the earnings goal to move to the next city. Three failed events and the chain closes.
